# Hesabaty Flutter English preview

Language can now be switched from the top-bar action or Settings. The choice
is stored locally and restored on next app launch. Material date pickers, RTL/LTR
direction and key navigation/sign-in labels follow the selected locale.

**Partial translation only:** many financial cards, dialogs, reports, SMS
review messages and recovery texts remain Arabic. Do not claim full English
localization, use as a preview and complete a proper ARB-based localization
review before Google Play launch.

UI language is an app-level preference and is not an excuse to delete or
rewrite imported finance data.
